# Product

## Register

product

## Users

College basketball coaches, general managers, and analysts who need to evaluate players and compare teams. They use this tool during scouting, recruiting, and game preparation. Their context ranges from quick in-game lookups to deep off-season analysis.

## Product Purpose

A college basketball analytics platform that makes advanced statistics accessible and actionable. It bridges the gap between raw data and decision-making by combining numbers with visual explanations. Success is when a coach can quickly understand a player's value through both metrics and intuitive visualizations, then dive deeper when needed.

## Brand Personality

Analytical, Pokemon, Simplistic

The "Pokemon" aspect means treating players as collectible characters with distinct identities, badges, and visual cards that make them memorable. The interface should feel like discovering and understanding player archetypes rather than just crunching numbers.

## Anti-references

- hoop-reference and BartTorvik: Too strictly analytical, dense with numbers, hard to understand at a glance
- Pure data tables without visual context
- Overly complex dashboards that require training to use

## Design Principles

1. **Show, then explain**: Visualizations first, numbers second, explanations third. Each metric should have an intuitive visual representation before the raw value.
2. **Progressive disclosure**: Quick lookups should be instant; deep analysis should be available but not overwhelming. Start simple, reveal complexity on demand.
3. **Character over data**: Players should feel like distinct characters with visual identities (cards, badges, cluster archetypes) rather than anonymous data points.
4. **Balance is key**: Never just numbers, never just visuals. Every insight should combine quantitative data with qualitative context.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. Consider color-blind users for data visualizations (use patterns/labels in addition to color). Ensure keyboard navigation works for all interactive elements. Reduced motion alternatives for all animations.
